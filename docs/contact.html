Open Positions at GHYAN Laboratory

We welcome enthusiastic and self-motivated researchers to join our lab in the Department of Physics at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.

1. Postdoctoral Positions

(IPDF / NPDF / Fellowships)
Open

We invite motivated postdoctoral candidates with background in experimental condensed matter physics, spintronics, magnetism, or computational micromagnetics/multiphysics.

  • Fellowships: Open to candidates holding or applying for ANRF/SERB N-PDF, Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship (IPDF), CSIR, UGC, or other national fellowships.
  • Form a Project: Prospective fellows are encouraged to contact the PI to jointly formulate and develop a competitive research project proposal.
Contact PI to Form a Project →

2. PhD Positions

(Institute Funded & JRF)
Open

We welcome applications from prospective PhD candidates interested in exploring spin, orbital, and hybrid quantum phenomena in thin films and heterostructures.

  • Funding Schemes: Positions are available through IIT (ISM) Institute Fellowships and external fellowships (CSIR-UGC JRF, DST-INSPIRE, or valid GATE/NET qualification).
  • Application: Prospective candidates should email Dr. Rahul Gupta and formally apply via the IIT (ISM) Dhanbad PhD Admission Portal.
Email PI → Admission Portal ↗

3. Master's Thesis Projects

(M.Sc. / M.Tech. Students)
Open

Opportunities are available for enthusiastic Master's students seeking semester projects or dissertation research in thin-film spintronics and numerical simulations.

  • Eligibility: M.Sc. in Physics / Applied Physics or M.Tech. in Materials Science, Nanotechnology, or related disciplines.
  • Scope: Hands-on experience with thin-film deposition, magnetotransport, FMR, or micromagnetic modeling (COMSOL, MuMax3).
Contact PI with CV →

4. Bachelor's Thesis & Internships

(B.Tech / B.Sc. Projects)
Open

Positions are open for motivated undergraduate students interested in semester-long thesis projects, capstone research, or summer/winter internships.

  • Duration: Typically 2–6 months (summer/winter internships or semester thesis projects).
  • Application: Interested undergraduate students must submit their application through our dedicated Google Form.
Apply via Google Form ↗

Welcome to GHYAN (ज्ञान)

The GHYAN (ज्ञान) Laboratory (Gupta Laboratory for Hybrid Quantum Systems and Spintronics) is a research group in the Department of Physics at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

Our research explores the interplay of quantum degrees of freedom, particularly spin and orbital angular momentum, in thin films, heterostructures, and hybrid systems. We investigate the fundamental mechanisms governing the generation, transport, conversion, and dynamics of quantum angular momentum and translate these concepts toward spintronics, orbitronics, hybrid quantum systems, quantum sensing, and emerging device technologies.

We employ an integrated theoretical, computational, and experimental approach, combining multiphysics and micromagnetic simulations using COMSOL, HFSS, and MuMax with materials development, thin-film fabrication, and electrical, magnetic, and ultrafast characterization.

🎓 We welcome highly motivated PhD students and postdoctoral researchers interested in working at the interface of quantum materials and emerging device technologies.

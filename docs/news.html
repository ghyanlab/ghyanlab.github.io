February 2025  •  Research Highlight & Press Release

Toward Sustainable Computing: Energy-Efficient SOT-MRAM Memory Innovation

A major breakthrough in Spin-Orbit Torque Magnetic Random-Access Memory (SOT-MRAM) led by Dr. Rahul Gupta and collaborators at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz (JGU) in partnership with Antaios has been featured internationally. Published in Nature Communications ("Harnessing orbital Hall effect in spin-orbit torque MRAM"), the research demonstrates an over 50% reduction in energy consumption by utilizing orbital currents rather than expensive, heavy metals.
